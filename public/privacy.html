Privacy Policy

Island Tower Tracker · Effective 28 November 2026

Island Tower Tracker ("the App") is an internal work app operated by Island Tower Electromechanical Works L.L.C ("Island Tower", "we") and used across the Island Tower group of companies to record daily site work: equipment usage, labour attendance, material transfers, internal vehicle trips and fuel requests. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Who can use the App

The App can be downloaded by anyone, but only accounts created by the company administrator can sign in. There is no public sign-up. Accounts are issued to the company's foremen, drivers, store staff and administrators.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, role (Foreman, Driver, Admin) and, where provided, a work phone number. These are set up by the administrator.
  • Work entries: the records you submit, such as job/site, dates, start and end times, working hours, trips, equipment, labour attendance, fuel quantities, remarks and the site location you type in.
  • Photos: pictures you take with the camera or choose from your gallery as proof of work (for example timesheets, equipment or vehicles). Photos are stamped with the date and time they were taken.
  • Basic technical data: information needed to keep you signed in and to send entries saved while offline once you are back online.

The App does not track your GPS location, read your contacts, messages or call logs, and does not show advertising.

How we use it

  • To record and approve daily site work, equipment and labour hours.
  • To prepare reports, invoices checks and payroll information for the company.
  • To let store staff and administrators approve or reject fuel requests and entries.

Your information is used only for these company operations. It is never sold or used for marketing.

Camera and photo access

The App asks for camera and photo access only when you choose to attach a photo to an entry. You can refuse or remove this permission at any time in your phone settings; some entries that require a photo cannot then be submitted.

Where your data is stored

Entries and account data are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase). Photos are stored with our image hosting provider (Cloudinary). Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only signed-in company accounts can access it, according to their role.

Who can see your data

Your entries can be seen by company administrators and, for fuel requests, by store staff. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

Work records are kept for as long as the company needs them for project, billing, payroll and legal purposes. When a staff member leaves, the administrator deactivates their account so it can no longer sign in.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask for your account to be closed or for your personal data to be deleted by contacting the administrator using the details below. Some work records may need to be kept for legal or accounting reasons, in which case your account will be deactivated and the records kept only as long as required.

Children

The App is a work tool for company employees and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
